# University of Michigan Library reference assets

Source and attribution: University of Michigan Library.

The 28 original floor-plan PDFs are unchanged. Their PNG companions were rasterized
for the local reference viewer. These staff-authored diagrams fall under the
Library's website-content CC-BY policy:
https://lib.umich.edu/about-us/policies/copyright-policy/

License linked by that policy: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Each original page and PDF URL is recorded in `floorplan_manifest.json`.
`photo_sources.json` records the research image URLs; only Shapiro exterior,
Shapiro Clark Commons, and Duderstadt exterior are bundled. Photographs with
identifiable people remain source links and are not redistributed in this branch.

The 3D interiors are separate authored illustrations. Source room names and floor
labels do not verify the generated wall positions, vertical spacing, circulation
cores, door openings, furniture, or accessibility. Rectangles in the catalogue
are approximate visual reference zones, not traced or surveyed room polygons.
